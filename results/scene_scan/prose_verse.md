# Henry VI scenes rescored with verse-only and prose-only models

Marlowe verse/prose from EMED line markup; Shakespeare verse/prose from Folger speech layout. Models trained on Marlowe vs Shakespeare (Henry VI family excluded) *within one mode*, so a prose scene is judged against prose models and a verse scene against verse models. ΔH = H(Marlowe) − H(Shakespeare) bits/char, negative favours Marlowe. The Marlowe prose corpus is small (mostly Faustus and the Jew of Malta), so prose-mode numbers are weak.
