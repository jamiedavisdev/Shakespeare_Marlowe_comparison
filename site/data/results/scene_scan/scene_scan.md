# Scene-level scan: Marlowe / Shakespeare / Peele / Greene

For each scanned play the models are trained with that play and any play sharing its plot/characters excluded from every author. P(·): multinomial function-word logistic regression (trained on 2000-word chunks, applied to the whole scene). ΔH(·) = H under that author's char model minus H under the Shakespeare char model, bits/char, proper nouns masked; negative = that author predicts the scene better than Shakespeare does. Scenes under ~400 words are weak evidence.
